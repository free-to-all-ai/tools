Michigan Medicare Savings Program Check

Michigan's Medicare Savings Programs pay the Medicare Part B premium, which is $202.90 a month in 2026, for people with lower incomes. The fullest one, QMB, also pays your Medicare deductibles and copays. Answer a few questions to see which program your income fits under Michigan's 2026 limits, what it would pay, and how to apply.

This is information, not legal or financial advice. Only the Michigan Department of Health and Human Services (MDHHS) can approve you, after you apply. Limits come from MDHHS's own policy manuals, checked on October 31, 2026. Nothing you type here leaves this page or gets saved.

How to apply

  1. Apply online at MI Bridges, in person, or on paper. MI Bridges is the fastest way and gives you proof of the date you applied. You can also apply at your local MDHHS office, which has to give you a paper application if you ask. There's no separate form for these programs. Michigan uses its regular health coverage application: the MDHHS-1171 Assistance Application with its health care coverage supplement, or the DCH-1426.
  2. Turning 65 soon? You can ask now. MDHHS will look at whether Medicaid would pay your Medicare premiums before you enroll in Medicare, even in the three months before your 65th birthday.
  3. Gather proof. Have your Medicare card, your Social Security benefit letter, pension or annuity statements, recent pay stubs if you work, recent bank statements, and any life insurance policies. MDHHS tells you in writing if it needs anything else.
  4. Watch for the decision. MDHHS's standard is to decide health coverage applications within 45 days, or 90 days when it has to decide whether you're disabled. MDHHS puts you in the program your income fits. You can't pick a different one.
  5. Expect the premium to stop coming out of your check, plus a refund. After you're approved, it usually takes Social Security about 120 days to stop taking the Part B premium out of your benefit. You get a refund for the premiums taken out while that was in process.
  6. If you're denied, you can ask for a hearing. You have 90 days from the date of the notice. The Benefits Cut Letter Deadlines tool works out your exact date and drafts the request.

Rules that protect you

Have QMB and still got a bill?

Doctors, hospitals, pharmacies, and labs can't bill people with QMB for Medicare deductibles, coinsurance, or copays. If one does, edit this letter, fill in the parts in [brackets], and send it to the billing office with a copy of your QMB or Medicaid card.

Free help

  • MMAP (Michigan Medicare/Medicaid Assistance Program): free, unbiased one-on-one Medicare counseling from trained counselors. They can help you apply. Call 1-800-803-7174.
  • MDHHS Beneficiary Help Line: questions about Michigan Medicaid and the Medicare buy-in. Call 1-800-642-3195.
  • Michigan Legal Help: Medicare Savings Programs explains each program in plain language.
  • Medicare.gov: Medicare Savings Programs has the federal limits and what each program covers.
  • Apply for Extra Help with drug costs on Social Security's site if you don't fit a program here. Extra Help has its own limits.
  • Detroit Hospital Financial Assistance Check if you already owe a hospital bill.

How this works. The tool adds up your monthly income the way Michigan's manual says to for these programs. It takes $20 off money you don't work for (or off your pay, if that's all you have). From pay for work it takes $65 and then half of the rest. If you're raising your own children under 18 at home, it first sets aside up to $498 a month for each one. The result is your countable income. Michigan's published limits already add that $20 back, so the tool compares your countable income to each limit minus $20. Savings are compared to the asset limit in MDHHS's Bridges Eligibility Manual (BEM 400).

In January, February, and March, MDHHS ignores the cost-of-living raise in your Social Security check and uses the December limits. Michigan updates the income limits each April 1 and the savings limits each January 1. Close to a limit? Apply anyway. MDHHS decides from your proof, and what it counts can differ from what you entered here.
