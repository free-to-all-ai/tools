Free legal help

  • Michigan Legal Help: Guide to Legal Help finds free legal aid near you, by county and problem.
  • Michigan Legal Help: Your Security Deposit has free forms and letters for getting a deposit back.
  • Detroit tenants with an eviction case: households earning up to twice the federal poverty level can get a free lawyer. At your 36th District Court hearing, tell the judge you want to talk to a free lawyer. City of Detroit, Office of Eviction Defense. Detroit Housing Resource helpline: (866) 313-2520.

How this works. Each answer comes from Michigan statutes, which were checked against the Michigan Legislature's official site on October 28, 2026 (the site says it's current through Public Act 114 of 2026). Green cards are what the law says directly. Amber cards depend on facts a judge decides. Red cards mean get a lawyer now.

Local ordinances, federal rules for public or subsidized housing, and your own lease can add more rights. Deadlines here are counted in calendar days from the dates you enter. Courts can count differently, so don't wait until the last day.
