How this works. Deadlines come from federal rules (29 CFR 2560.503-1 and 45 CFR 147.136) and Michigan's Patient's Right to Independent Review Act. They were checked against official sources on October 28, 2026. Green cards are what the rule says directly. Amber cards depend on your plan. Red cards mean act now.

Some older "grandfathered" plans don't have to follow every rule here. Deadlines are counted in calendar days from the date you enter. Your denial letter must list your own deadlines, and if it gives you more time, use that.
