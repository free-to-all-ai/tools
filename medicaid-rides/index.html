Michigan Medicaid Free Rides Finder

Michigan Medicaid and the Healthy Michigan Plan give free rides to the doctor, dentist, pharmacy, lab, therapy, dialysis, and mental health or recovery visits. If you have a car or a friend can drive you, your plan can pay you back for the miles instead. Pick the plan on your card to see the number to call, how far ahead to book, and the call-by date for your appointment.

This is information, not legal or medical advice. Every phone number and booking rule here was checked on each plan's own website and the state's list on October 31, 2026. Plans change ride companies, so if a number doesn't work, call the member services number on your card. Nothing you type here leaves this page or gets saved.

Emergency? Call 911. These rides are for appointments, not emergencies.

If your ride is late or doesn't come

  1. Call the ride line right away. Use the number you booked with and give your trip number. Most ride companies have a "Where's my ride" option. Plans must tell you about delays and find you another ride when one falls through.
  2. Call your doctor's office. Tell them you're on your way or that the ride didn't come, so they can hold or move your visit.
  3. Call your plan's member services and ask to file a complaint. Every plan has to have a complaint process for rides. Write down the date, the time, who you talked to, and what they said. If you missed your visit, ask them to set up a ride for the new date.
  4. Still stuck? Call the state. The MDHHS Beneficiary Help Line is (800) 642-3195, Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 7 PM.
  5. Got a letter denying a ride? You have a right to a hearing. Plan members first appeal to the plan. See Benefits Cut Letter Deadlines for the deadlines, and keep the letter.

Other help

  • Don't know your plan, or want to change plans? Call MI Enrolls at (800) 975-7630 (TTY (888) 263-5897), Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 7 PM. You can also see your plan in your MI Bridges account.
  • Kids' dental visits under Healthy Kids Dental: the ride comes from your Medicaid health plan's ride line. With no plan, it comes from ModivCare or your local office, same as above.
  • Rides to some community mental health services may come from your local Community Mental Health agency instead. Ask your case manager.
  • No plan and not in Wayne, Oakland, or Macomb? Find your county's MDHHS office on the state's county office map.
  • On Medicare too? The Michigan Medicare Savings Program Check shows if the state can pay your Part B premium and Medicare copays.
  • A medical bill after a ride or an ambulance? The Detroit Medical Bill Check shows what Medicare pays for each code.

How this works. Each plan's ride number, ride company, and booking notice come from the plan's own member page or member handbook, checked against the Michigan Department of Health and Human Services list of health plan ride contacts (revised October 1, 2026). Where the two differ, we show the plan's own words first and say what the state's list adds. The call-by date counts back from your appointment: business days skip Saturdays and Sundays, and "72 hours" counts back three calendar days.

Not covered by these rides: people with Emergency Services Only Medicaid or Plan First, people who haven't met a spend-down yet, and nursing home residents, whose home arranges routine rides. Trips that aren't for a covered service, like the grocery store, aren't covered either, though HAP CareSource adds some food pantry trips.
