DatesMinimum wageAge 16 or 17Tipped cash wageTips must cover

All rates are per hour. "Tips must cover" is the gap your tips have to fill for an employer to pay the tipped cash wage. A new worker under 20 can be paid a $4.25 training wage for their first 90 days (MCL 408.934b). Starting in 2028, the minimum wage goes up with inflation each January unless Michigan's unemployment rate was 8.5% or higher the year before (MCL 408.934). The tipped wage rises to 44% of the minimum in 2028 and keeps climbing to 50% in 2031 (MCL 408.934d).

How to file a wage claim in Michigan

  1. Gather what you have. Pay stubs, a schedule or time records, texts about hours or pay, and your own notes of the hours you worked. The state asks for pay statements and a record of hours worked (LEO: Filing a Minimum Wage or Overtime Complaint).
  2. File with the Wage and Hour Division. It's free.
    • Online, the fastest way: michigan.gov/wageclaim
    • Paper: form WHD-9430. Mail it to Wage & Hour Division, P.O. Box 30476, Lansing, MI 48909-7976, fax it to 517-763-0110, or email it to WHCLAIM@MICHIGAN.GOV.
    • In person, by appointment only: 3026 W. Grand Blvd., Suite 2-750, Detroit (Mondays and Wednesdays), or 320 S. Walnut St., Lansing.
    • Questions, or to ask for a form: 1-855-464-9243. Forms come in English, Spanish, and Arabic.
  3. Fill in the amounts. The form asks for the period of your claim, then minimum wage, overtime, and hourly wages owed, and a total gross amount. The summary above gives you each one. Paste it into the part of the form that asks you to describe your claim.
  4. What happens next. The state investigates and your employer has to hand over its records. The state can't promise you'll get paid. That depends on things like whether the business closed or has money. Tell the division about any payment you get and any change to your phone or address.
  5. The federal option. You can also call the U.S. Department of Labor's Wage and Hour Division at 1-866-487-9243. It keeps complaints confidential and says it "enforces the law without regard to an employee's immigration status" (DOL FAQ). Federal claims must be brought within 2 years, or 3 if the violation was willful (29 U.S.C. 255).

Sources: LEO: Online Employment Wage Complaint Form, the WHD-9430 form (revised 2/2025), and LEO: Minimum Wage and Overtime FAQ.

How this works. For each week, the tool looks up the Michigan minimum wage in effect on the first day of that week. It requires at least that much for the first 40 hours, and 1.5 times your regular rate for every hour after 40. Your regular rate is the higher of the minimum wage and any rate you were promised. Then it subtracts what you were paid. For tipped jobs, it counts your tips toward the minimum the way the state's FAQ describes. Green cards are what the law says directly. Amber cards depend on facts the state or a judge decides. Red cards mean act now.

Deadlines are counted from the last day of each workweek. Your payday comes later, so your real deadline may be a few days later than shown. Don't wait until the last day.
