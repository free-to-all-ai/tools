Free to All AI

Free help with Michigan bills, benefits, and rights

Small, free tools for people in Detroit and across Michigan. Each one does one job, gives you an answer you can check, and links to the law or data the answer comes from.

No sign-up and no tracking. What you type stays on your own device. These tools give information, not legal, medical, or tax advice.

All tools

  • Michigan · Renters

    Michigan Renter Rights Check

    Michigan tenant rights for getting your security deposit back, eviction notices, repairs, lockouts, illegal lease clauses, and landlord retaliation. Works out your deadlines from your dates.

  • Michigan · SNAP · Medicaid

    Benefits Cut Letter Deadlines

    Got an MDHHS letter that denies, cuts, or stops food assistance (SNAP), Medicaid, cash, or child care help? See your deadline to ask for a hearing and to keep your benefits while you wait. Drafts the hearing request.

  • Detroit area · Medical bills

    Detroit Medical Bill Check

    Type the CPT or HCPCS codes from an itemized medical bill and see the 2026 Medicare rate for each in the Detroit area. Flags charges billed twice and drafts a letter to dispute them.

  • Detroit area · Hospital bills

    Detroit Hospital Financial Assistance Check

    Can't pay a hospital bill? Most big Detroit-area hospitals have a financial assistance (charity care) policy that cuts or erases bills under set income limits. See how Henry Ford, Corewell Health (Beaumont), Trinity Health, McLaren, DMC, and Michigan Medicine would likely treat you.

  • Health insurance

    Insurance Denial Appeal Helper

    Health insurance denied a claim or a prior authorization? See your appeal deadlines, including Michigan's independent external review, and get an appeal letter you can edit and send.

  • Michigan · Wages

    Michigan Back Pay Check

    Shorted on pay? See Michigan's minimum wage by date, work out unpaid minimum wage, overtime, and promised pay week by week, and get the steps to file a free state wage claim.

  • Uber · Lyft · DoorDash · Instacart

    Gig Driver True Pay Calculator

    Is driving for Uber, Lyft, or DoorDash worth it after gas? Enter your pay, hours, and miles to see what you really made per hour after car costs, plus a tax set-aside.

  • Michigan · Criminal records

    Michigan Clean Slate Expungement Check

    Can your Michigan record be expunged? List your convictions and dates to see whether each may be set aside under the Clean Slate law, whether it clears automatically after 7 or 10 years or needs an application to the court, and when.

  • Scams · Calls · Texts · Emails

    Is This a Scam? Check the Warning Signs

    Got a call, text, email, or pop-up that feels wrong, or worried a parent is being scammed? Check off what happened to see which FTC and FBI warning signs it matches, what to do right away, and who to call in Michigan.

  • Michigan · Medicare

    Michigan Medicare Savings Program Check

    On Medicare with a lower income? Michigan can pay your $202.90 a month Part B premium, and with QMB your Medicare deductibles and copays too. See if you fit QMB, SLMB, QI, or QDWI under the 2026 income and savings limits, and how to apply.

  • Michigan · Unemployment

    Michigan Unemployment Protest Deadline Check

    UIA denied your unemployment, cut it, or says you were overpaid? See the last day UIA must receive your protest or appeal, counting weekends, holidays, and the Nov. 2 to 9, 2026 switch to MiUI. Shows where to fax or mail it and drafts the letter.

  • Michigan · Tax credits

    Michigan Tax Credits You Might Be Missing

    Estimate the federal Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC), Michigan's Working Families Tax Credit, the Homestead Property Tax Credit for renters and owners, and the Home Heating Credit. Also shows earlier years you can still claim, like a missed 2023 EITC until April 15, 2027.

  • Michigan · College

    Free College in Michigan: Who Qualifies?

    Can community college be tuition-free for you? Check Michigan Reconnect (now ages 21 and up), the Achievement Scholarship's Community College Guarantee for recent graduates, TIP if you had Medicaid as a kid, and the Detroit Promise. See which rule fits you or stops you, and what to do next.

Why "Free to All"

Three words are carved over the main entrance of the Boston Public Library: FREE TO ALL. Anyone could walk in and use the most powerful information tools of the day, whoever they were and whatever they could pay. AI is the most powerful information tool of our day. These tools are that promise, rewritten for now.

How the tools work

  • Nothing leaves your device. No accounts, no tracking, no servers. What you type stays on your screen.
  • Every answer cites its source. Each result links to the law or data it came from and lists the date it was checked.
  • Information, not advice. The tools explain your rights and the rules. They tell you when to get a lawyer or other help, and where to find it free.
  • One file each. Save any tool's page and it works offline.

Free and open source. See the code, report a mistake, or suggest a tool on GitHub.
