Benefits Cut Letter Deadlines

MDHHS sent you a letter that denies, lowers, or stops your benefits. You have the right to ask for a hearing. Enter the date printed on the letter to see two deadlines: the one to keep your benefits coming while you wait, and the last day to ask for a hearing at all.

This is information, not legal advice. Your notice lists your hearing rights. If it shows an earlier date than this page, go by your notice. Nothing you type here leaves this page or gets saved.

How this works. Deadlines come from the Michigan Department of Health and Human Services (MDHHS) Bridges Administrative Manual: BAM 600, Hearings (version dated May 1, 2026), BAM 220, Case Actions (August 1, 2026), and BAM 210, Redetermination (October 1, 2026). Federal rules back them up: 7 CFR 273.15 for SNAP and 42 CFR 431.221 to 431.231 for Medicaid. All were checked against the official sources on October 30, 2026.

Days are calendar days, counted starting the day after the date on your notice. When the 90th day lands on a weekend or a State of Michigan holiday, the deadline moves to the next business day. Holidays follow Michigan Civil Service Regulation 5.08. Green cards are what the rule says directly. Amber cards depend on your facts. Red cards mean act now.

This page doesn't cover overpayment notices, fraud (intentional program violation) cases, or Social Security disability decisions, which have their own rules. Free and open source. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere or stored.
