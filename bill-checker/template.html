Detroit Medical Bill Check

Got an itemized bill with codes on it? Type each line below and see what Medicare pays a doctor for the same service in Wayne, Oakland, Macomb, or Washtenaw County this year. It's a yardstick for spotting charges worth asking about, and it flags lines that look billed twice.

This is information, not medical, legal, or financial advice. Medicare's rate isn't what you owe. Your insurer's contract or the hospital's own policy decides that. Use these numbers to ask better questions. Nothing you type here leaves this page or gets saved.

Medicare rate = 2026 Medicare Physician Fee Schedule amount for Michigan locality 01 (Detroit) times units, or the 2026 Clinical Lab Fee Schedule for lab tests. Many private insurers pay more than Medicare, often 1.5 to 2.5 times. A line at 5 times Medicare or more is worth asking about. A hospital's separate facility fee (rows with 4-digit revenue codes and no CPT code) is paid differently and isn't checked here.

Other things worth knowing

  • Nonprofit hospitals must have a financial assistance policy. Most large Detroit-area hospitals are nonprofits. Federal tax law requires them to have a written financial assistance policy and limits what they can charge people who qualify for it to the amounts generally billed to insured patients. Ask the billing office for the policy and an application. 26 U.S.C. 501(r) IRS
  • Paid without insurance and got a Good Faith Estimate? If a provider charged you at least $400 more than its estimate, you can dispute the bill through CMS within 120 calendar days of the bill date. There is a $25 fee. CMS: dispute a bill
  • Surprise bills from out-of-network doctors at an in-network hospital or in an emergency are limited by the No Surprises Act. CMS medical bill rights
  • Check any rate yourself in CMS's own lookup tool. Pick "Pricing information," year 2026, and locality "Detroit." CMS fee schedule lookup
Where the numbers come from
  • 2026 Medicare Physician Fee Schedule, October release (RVU26D, updated 08/26/2026), non-QPP conversion factor $33.4009. CMS RVU26D
  • 2026 geographic adjustments for Michigan locality 01, Detroit (work 1.000, practice expense 0.965, malpractice 1.686), from the GPCI file in the same release. Imaging technical components use the lower OPPS cap where CMS lists one.
  • 2026 Clinical Laboratory Fee Schedule, fourth quarter (26CLABQ4). CMS 26CLABQ4
  • Formula: (work RVU × work GPCI + practice expense RVU × PE GPCI + malpractice RVU × MP GPCI) × conversion factor. Clinicians in qualifying alternative payment models get a slightly higher factor ($33.5675), about half a percent more.
  • Not covered: drugs and supplies (most J and A codes), anesthesia time, hospital facility fees, ambulance, and dental. Code descriptions aren't shown because the American Medical Association owns them. Your bill lists what each code means.
  • Data pulled from cms.gov on 2026-10-28. Rates change each January.
