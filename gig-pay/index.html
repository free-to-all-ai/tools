Gig Driver True Pay Calculator

Your app shows what it paid you. It doesn't show what the driving cost you. Enter the numbers from your app screens and your odometer to see what you really made per hour after car costs. Works for Uber, Lyft, DoorDash, Instacart, Spark, and any other app that pays you to drive.

This is information, not tax or financial advice. The math is exact. The car cost is an estimate unless you enter your own. Nothing you type leaves this page or gets saved.

How this works. True pay per hour is your pay and tips, minus car costs (miles times cost per mile) and other work costs, divided by every hour you worked. Green cards are math from your own numbers. Amber cards are estimates or depend on your situation. Red cards are money you may owe or may be losing.

Sources, checked October 30, 2026. IRS standard mileage rates: 67 cents a mile for 2024, 70 cents for 2025, 72.5 cents from January 1 to June 30, 2026 (Notice 2026-10), and 76 cents from July 1, 2026 (Announcement 2026-11), per the IRS rate table. Self-employment tax: 15.3% of 92.35% of net profit, from the IRS self-employment tax page and Schedule SE. Michigan minimum wage: MCL 408.934.

The IRS usually announces next year's mileage rate in December. For dates after 2026, this page uses the latest rate until it's updated. The tax numbers are a rough set-aside for Social Security and Medicare only. They leave out income tax, credits, and anything else on your return.
